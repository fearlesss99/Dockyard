## Identity Substitution Matrix

Each identity-bearing field was individually tested to prove
substitution is rejected before any scheduler mutation write.

| # | Field | Defense Layer | Rejection Mechanism |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | `task_id` | `decide_reconciliation` / `_run_defense` | `receipt_task_mismatch` — queue_entry task_id vs receipt task_id mismatch |
| 2 | `revision` | `RecoveryRequest.__post_init__` | `identity_mismatch` — `queue_entry.revision != canonical_task.revision` |
| 3 | `attempt` (failed, ≥4) | `RecoveryRequest.__post_init__` | `attempt_four` — `retry_budget_used >= 3` |
| 4 | `canonical dispatch event_id` | `RecoveryExecutionRequest.__post_init__` | `canonical_event` — `decision.canonical_dispatch_event_id != expected` |
| 5 | `recovery_generation` | `RecoveryExecutionRequest.__post_init__` | `recovery_generation` — `expected != request.recovery_generation` |
| 6 | `decision_replay_digest` | `RecoveryExecutionRequest.__post_init__` | `decision_digest` — `decision.replay_digest != expected` |
| 7 | `execution_id` | `RecoveryExecutionRequest.__post_init__` | `execution_id` — `_text()` rejects empty or non-string |
| 8 | `content_digest` | `RecoveryExecutionReceipt.__post_init__` | `content_digest_mismatch` — SHA-256 comparison |
| 9 | `dispatch_id` | `RecoveryExecutionRequest.__post_init__` | `canonical_event` — mismatch between `expected_canonical_dispatch_event_id` and `decision.canonical_dispatch_event_id` |

**Result**: Every identity field substitution is caught before the
executor enters the store lock.  No scheduler mutation can occur with a
substituted identity.
